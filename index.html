Academic Excellence

Department of CSE (AIML)

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at the Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata, provides an innovative 4-year B.Tech curriculum uniting computer architecture, mathematics, deep learning, NLP, and intelligent robotics.

Events, workshops, and hackathons are stewarded by an energetic student organizing committee working under dedicated faculty advisory to cultivate industry-ready technical skills and leadership.
